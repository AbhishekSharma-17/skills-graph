# Style Archetypes — Reference Router

Style archetypes are complete aesthetic systems. Choose one as your foundation, then adapt. Each archetype includes typography, color, layout, component, and motion specifications. When no archetype is chosen, default to the general design principles in the build references (04-10).

## Sub-References

| Reference | File | Read When |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| **Minimalist Editorial** | `references/style-archetypes/minimalist.md` | Clean, editorial, warm monochrome, document-style, Notion/Linear aesthetic |
| **Brutalist Industrial** | `references/style-archetypes/brutalist-industrial.md` | Raw, mechanical, Swiss typography, military terminal, data-heavy dashboards |
| **High-End Agency** | `references/style-archetypes/high-end-agency.md` | $150k+ agency feel, ethereal glass, editorial luxury, premium animations |
| **Creative Arsenal** | `references/style-archetypes/creative-arsenal.md` | Advanced patterns, Bento grids, scroll animations, hero paradigms, magnetic UI |

## Quick Selection Guide

- **Minimalist Editorial** — Workspace tools, documentation sites, content platforms, knowledge bases. When the product should feel calm, typographic, and Notion-like.
- **Brutalist Industrial** — Data dashboards, analytics platforms, developer portfolios, editorial sites that need to feel like declassified blueprints or mechanical readouts.
- **High-End Agency** — Marketing sites, SaaS landing pages, agency portfolios, luxury brand sites. When the output must read as a $150k agency build with cinematic motion.
- **Creative Arsenal** — Feature sections, interactive showcases, product demos, scroll-heavy storytelling pages. When you need specific advanced patterns (Bento 2.0, scroll hijack, magnetic UI) rather than a full aesthetic system.

## Usage Notes

- Archetypes override general references (04-10) where they conflict. For example, Minimalist Editorial permits specific serif fonts that the general typography reference discourages.
- You may combine one primary archetype with individual patterns from Creative Arsenal. Do not mix two primary archetypes (Minimalist + Brutalist would conflict).
- Creative Arsenal is a pattern library, not a full aesthetic. Pair it with one of the other three archetypes or with the general references.
